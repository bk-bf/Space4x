Space4x rule: build only what is named, by the user, the issue or the validated mockup (mockup/sketch). When implementing an issue, take every figure, label, rule and screen element from the issue and the mockup; where they leave something open or contradict each other, do not build that part and do not guess: leave it out, keep the rest working, and list what you left open in a comment on the issue and in your report. Do not ask the user what the issue or mockup already answers. When designing (mockups and sketches), a mechanic, building, screen element, figure, name, number or rule the user has not asked for is a question first: ask with AskUserQuestion, wait for the answer, then build. Removing or renaming something nobody named is left out the same way.
