# Gate chain: Earth today to a stabilised jump point

Ids refer to `catalogue.csv`. "New" lists only ids first needed at that milestone. Ids marked **[thin]** belong to the thin playable path (see the end).

Tier: P proven today, E engineering-plausible, S physics-permitted but speculative.

## 1. Reusable orbital launch (P)
- Unlocks: cheap, repeated lift from Earth; every later milestone is paid for in launches until 12.
- New: iron [thin], chromium, nickel, stainless_steel [thin], aluminium [thin], lithium, copper [thin], al_li_alloy [thin], nickel_superalloy [thin], grcop, oxygen [thin], lox [thin], methane, lch4 [thin], rp1, helium4 [thin], carbon_fibre, epoxy, cfrp [thin], titanium [thin], ti_6al_4v
- Buildings: mine_earth, smelter, alloy_works, air_separation_plant, cryogenic_liquefaction_plant, composite_works, rocket_factory, launch_site
- Components: chemical_engine_methalox, kerolox_engine, propellant_tank, copv, structure_truss
- Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Falcon_9

## 2. Orbital propellant transfer (E)
- Unlocks: refuelling in LEO, so a ship can leave LEO with full tanks; first delta-v gate beyond LEO.
- New: polyimide [thin], pet_film, gold
- Buildings: propellant_depot
- Components: propellant_tank (cryogenic, with mli_blanket), docking adapter
- Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Orbital_propellant_depot

## 3. Long-duration LEO station with regenerative life support (P)
- Unlocks: crew stays for months; habitat module tech; water and oxygen recycling.
- New: water [thin], hydrogen [thin], carbon_dioxide [thin], zeolite, amine_sorbent, lioh, platinum, ammonia, aramid, alumina, nextel, fused_silica, silicone
- Buildings: life_support_plant, electrolysis_plant, sabatier_plant
- Components: habitat_module, life_support_unit, whipple_shield, radiator
- Source: https://www.esa.int/Science_Exploration/Human_and_Robotic_Exploration/Research/Advanced_Closed_Loop_System

## 4. Space solar power arrays at scale (P)
- Unlocks: tens to hundreds of kW per ship and station.
- New: silicon [thin], electronic_silicon [thin], gallium, arsenic, indium, phosphorus, germanium, silver, gaas, ingap, mj_solar_cell [thin], si_solar_cell, li_ion_cell [thin], cobalt, graphite
- Buildings: solar_cell_fab, semiconductor_fab, battery_plant
- Components: solar_array, battery_pack
- Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Multi-junction_solar_cell

## 5. Data centres and autonomy (P)
- Unlocks: compute and AI as resources; autonomous robots where light delay rules out teleoperation (asteroids, outer system).
- New: neon, fluorite, ai_accelerator, rad_hard_ic, compute [thin], ai_models [thin]
- Buildings: semiconductor_fab, data_centre
- Components: avionics
- Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/AI_accelerator

## 6. Crewed lunar landing and surface outpost (P)
- Unlocks: sorties to the lunar south pole; first lunar habitat.
- New: lh2 [thin], uhmwpe [thin], ethylene, pica, phenolic_resin
- Buildings: launch_site (lunar lander pad)
- Components: chemical_engine_hydrolox, heat_shield, habitat_module, radiation_shield
- Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Artemis_Base_Camp

## 7. Fission surface power (E)
- Unlocks: continuous power through the 14-day lunar night and later at Mars and beyond.
- New: uranium, haleu [thin], heu, molybdenum, u_mo_fuel, sodium, haynes_230, tungsten, beryllium, boron, boron_carbide
- Buildings: enrichment_plant, fuel_fabrication_plant, fission_reactor

- Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kilopower

## 8. Lunar polar ice mining (E)
- Unlocks: lunar water, hence local oxygen and hydrogen propellant.
- New: (uses water, hydrogen, oxygen) lunar_regolith [thin]
- Buildings: ice_mine, regolith_excavator, electrolysis_plant
- Components: none new
- Source: https://www.sciencenews.org/article/moon-crash-reveals-crater-held-water

## 9. Regolith oxygen and metals (E)
- Unlocks: oxygen from dry regolith anywhere on the Moon; iron and silicon as by-products.
- New: ilmenite, anorthite, ysz, iron (now from regolith)
- Buildings: regolith_oxygen_plant, smelter
- Components: none new
- Source: https://ntrs.nasa.gov/citations/20110008535

## 10. Regolith construction (E)
- Unlocks: shielded habitats, landing pads and berms without lift from Earth.
- New: sintered_regolith [thin], sulfur, sulfur_concrete, basalt, basalt_fibre
- Buildings: sinter_printer, fibre_works

## 11. Permanent Moon base (E, about 2100 in the premise)
- Unlocks: closed food loop and a self-sustaining crew; game pacing after this point.
- New: plant_biomass [thin], potassium, nitrogen, spirulina
- Buildings: greenhouse, life_support_plant
- Components: habitat_module, inflatable_habitat (vectran)
- Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moon_Village

## 12. Cislunar propellant economy (E)
- Unlocks: lunar-made LOX/LH2 sold at depots at L1/L2 and in LEO; Earth lift no longer limits propellant.
- New: none (lox, lh2 now sourced off Earth)
- Buildings: propellant_depot, cryogenic_liquefaction_plant (lunar)
- Components: propellant_tank
- Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lunar_water

## 13. Nuclear electric propulsion (E)
- Unlocks: high-Isp cargo tugs for asteroids and Mars; low thrust, long trips.

- Buildings: magnet_works
- Components: hall_thruster, ion_thruster, fission_reactor_ship, radiator
- Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nuclear_electric_rocket

## 14. Nuclear thermal propulsion (E)
